## Key Observations for KDMC Copywriting

The Ruimsig site uses very brief, functional copy — most doctor bios are one sentence or less. This is an opportunity for KDMC to stand out with **warmer, more personal language**. Specific copy improvements to consider for krugersdorpmedicalcentre.co.za:

1. **Hero headline** — Ruimsig uses a generic welcome message. KDMC's "Your Health. Our Purpose." is already stronger. Consider reinforcing with a community/trust angle: *"Serving Krugersdorp families since 1994."*

2. **Doctor bios** — Ruimsig bios are 1–2 sentences. KDMC can differentiate with fuller bios that mention years of experience, specific conditions treated, and a personal touch (e.g. born and raised in the area).

3. **Services** — Ruimsig lists 3 services with minimal descriptions. KDMC has 6 services with more detail — lean into this breadth. Highlight the **Centre for Diabetes**, **Skin Specialist**, and **Mental Health & Psychiatry** as differentiators not present at Ruimsig.

4. **Staff section** — Both practices show their support staff, which builds trust. KDMC's staff section is well-executed.

5. **Conditions page** — Ruimsig has a "Condition information" nav link but the page returns 404 (broken). This is a real opportunity: a working, well-written Conditions page at KDMC would be a meaningful differentiator.

6. **Blog** — Ruimsig has a "Dr. Jurgens' Blog" nav link (also 404). Dr. Jurgens' blog could be a genuine trust-builder at KDMC if activated — even 4–6 posts per year on relevant health topics.
